When choosing macro lenses, there are two things that must be considered.
Magnification ratio and minimum focusing distance.
Macro lenses have a one-to-one magnification ratio,
meaning the size of the image captured is the same size as in real life.
Other macro lenses can have a 5 to 1 magnification ratio,

In regards to minimum focusing distance,
this is simply how close or how far you need to be
to your subject to stay in focus.
