# Project brief — THE GRANT RUN, Ep. 1 (trailer reel)

## Outcome
- Project name: The Grant Run — Ep. 1 trailer (working title)
- Audience: 18–30 car-culture / street-racing / Atlanta audience on IG and TikTok; fans of irreverent animated-comedy storytelling
- What should the viewer know, feel, or do? Know Gordon and Trixie in 40 s; feel the adrenaline and the "wait, WHAT?" of the secret; follow for Ep. 2
- Central promise: Gordon Grant has a bloodline he doesn't know about — and it's about to catch up with him
- CTA: "EP. 2 →" end card + follow (in-app caption)

## Deliverables
- Platform and placement: Instagram Reels + TikTok (same 9:16 master; per-platform caption copy)
- Duration: 40.0 s target (hook ≤ 2 s; loopable)
- Aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate: 9:16, 1080×1920, **24 fps** (all hero sources are native 24 fps; converting to 30 would add judder)
- Language and caption treatment: English; burned-in text cards + burned captions for the 4 spoken lines; SRT sidecar kept
- Variants: master only for now; series template reusable for Ep. 2+

## Creative
- Tone/reference: Rick-and-Morty-style structure (cold open mid-chaos, deadpan character cards, escalation, hard tonal drop, half-revealed secret, stinger) — structure and attitude only, no characters, audio or visuals from that show
- Look: gritty phone/dashcam/handheld — light grain, slightly desaturated, crushed blacks, subtle handheld drift, REC/timestamp only on phone shots
- Characters: Gordon Grant (23, Atlanta, anti-authority, street racer); Michelle "Trixie" (navigator)
- Lineage: secretly descended from a famous racing family — **never named or announced** on screen
- Music/SFX: engine/SFX from `assets/originals/sfx_zr1x/` + the clips' own audio; music TBD (see open questions)
- AI-generated media: source footage is AI-generated (supplied by the user)
